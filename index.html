About

Kristian Droste is a neuroscientist and product developer who builds AI systems. He runs IntoMind, a neurotechnology company building noninvasive brain-computer interfaces, where his own work includes neural foundation models embedded on wearable EEG devices. kdro.ai is his personal studio: a small sample of his work worth sharing.

Everything here is built the same way: plain code, no frameworks, no tracking.
